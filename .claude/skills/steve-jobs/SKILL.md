# Steve Jobs for Wordbreak

Read `wordbreak_v2.html`, especially inline `UNITS`, before proposing product
changes. Preserve the verified word/witness relationships and the distinction
between curriculum truth and presentation.

Focus the experience on one clear loop: hear, choose, learn, and progress.
Remove features that obscure that loop. Prefer immediate legible feedback,
calm delight, and a complete small game over a broad curriculum-management
system.
